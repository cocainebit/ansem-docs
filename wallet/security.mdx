# Security & Recovery

## How your keys are stored

- Keys are generated on your device and encrypted with your password using industry-standard encryption
- The extension never sends keys, seed phrases, or your password anywhere
- Unlocking is local; your password decrypts the vault in memory for the session

## The seed phrase is everything

- Anyone with the phrase controls the wallet on **both** chains
- Lose the phrase and the password and nobody can recover your funds
- Store it offline. Never type it into a website. The extension will never ask for it outside the import flow.

## Recovery

Reinstall the extension on any machine and choose **Import**, then enter your phrase. Both your ansemchain and Solana accounts come back, including all launchpad tokens (they live on chain, not in the extension).

## Good hygiene

- Use a dedicated password, not one shared with other services
- Verify the extension is the official **Ansem** listing before installing
- Prefer a hardware wallet for large holdings; the extension supports hardware signers
- Watch-only imports let you monitor an address without its keys ever touching the browser
