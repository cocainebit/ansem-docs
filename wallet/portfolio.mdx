# Your Portfolio

The home screen shows everything you hold, on both chains, in one list with live USD values.

## ansemchain side

- **CHANSE** - your native gas and staking balance, priced from the on-chain oracle
- **ANSEM** - the bridged voucher token (1:1 with the pump.fun SPL), tradeable and bridgeable but not used for gas
- **Launchpad tokens** - every curve token you hold appears automatically with its name, ticker, and image. The wallet discovers them straight from the launchpad contract; there is nothing to add manually.

## Solana side

- **SOL** and your SPL tokens, including the official ANSEM and CHANSE SPLs
- Token names, icons, and prices resolve from the standard Solana token lists; CHANSE's USD price comes from the same oracle the chain uses, so both sides of your portfolio agree

## Sending and receiving

Each side has its own send flow and address format:

- ansemchain addresses start with `ansem1…`
- Solana addresses are base58

Always match the chain to the address. To move CHANSE or ANSEM *between* the chains, use the built-in [bridge](/wallet/bridging), not a plain send.
