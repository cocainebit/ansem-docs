# Bridging from the Wallet

The bridge is multi-asset. From the wallet you can move **CHANSE** or **ANSEM** between Solana and ansemchain:

- **Solana -> ansemchain**: your SPL token is locked in that asset's bridge vault on Solana and the same amount of the matching native denom is minted to your ansemchain address (CHANSE mints `uchanse`, ANSEM mints `uansem`)
- **ansemchain -> Solana**: your native balance is escrowed and the same amount of SPL is released from that asset's vault to your Solana address

Total supply of each asset across both chains stays constant. Nothing is wrapped. CHANSE on ansemchain is the native gas and staking token; ANSEM is a bridged asset. Both are SPL Token-2022 mints on Solana, which the wallet handles for you.

## To ansemchain

<Steps>

### Open *Bridge to ansemchain*

Pick the asset (**CHANSE** or **ANSEM**). The wallet shows your SPL balance for that asset on Solana.

### Enter the amount and recipient

Your own ansemchain address is pre-filled. The first deposit registers your recipient on chain; after that it is fixed for your Solana account and shared across all assets. The amount is capped by the asset's `max_mint_per_tx`.

### Confirm

You pay only the Solana network fee (~0.000005 SOL), or nothing if the relayer sponsors it. The relayer detects the deposit and mints the matching denom to your ansemchain address, typically in under a minute.

</Steps>

## To Solana

<Steps>

### Open *Bridge to Solana*

Pick the asset (**CHANSE** or **ANSEM**). The wallet shows your native balance for that asset.

### Enter the amount and the Solana recipient

The wallet shows the asset's `max_burn_per_tx` and warns you if the amount crosses its `admin_approval_threshold`.

### Confirm

The withdrawal costs a small CHANSE gas fee on ansemchain (~0.0075 CHANSE) even when you are withdrawing ANSEM, since CHANSE is the gas token. Your coins are **escrowed** (not burned yet); the relayer releases SPL to your Solana address, typically in under a minute after the withdrawal confirms. Once the release lands, the escrow settles.

</Steps>

## Limits and safety

Per-asset limits and rate caps are enforced by the bridge module: `max_mint_per_tx`, `max_burn_per_tx`, and an `admin_approval_threshold` above which large withdrawals need admin approval before release. See [Bridge fees and limits](/bridge/fees-and-limits) for the fields and current values. If a transfer ever stalls past a few minutes, it has not vanished: escrowed coins are refunded if a release fails, the relayer retries, and every deposit and withdrawal is traceable on both explorers.
