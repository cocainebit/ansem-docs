# The Ansem Wallet

The Ansem wallet is a browser extension that manages both sides of the ecosystem from one seed phrase:

- **ansemchain** - your CHANSE (the native gas and staking token), your ANSEM, every launchpad token you hold, proposals, and chain transactions
- **Solana** - your SOL, the ANSEM and CHANSE SPL tokens, and the bridge between the two worlds

## What it does

- Hold, send, and receive CHANSE and ANSEM on both chains
- Bridge CHANSE and ANSEM between Solana and ansemchain in about a minute, from inside the wallet
- See every launchpad token you own with its image and live USD value
- Track your full portfolio across both chains in one view
- Connect to the launchpad, proposals site, and other Ansem apps

## Self-custodial

Your keys are generated locally and encrypted with your password. They never leave your device. Anyone with your seed phrase controls your funds; nobody without it can touch them, including us.

## Always current, no updates needed

The wallet reads the protocol's live configuration from an on-chain registry. When contract addresses or the official mint rotate, the wallet follows automatically within minutes. You don't need to update the extension for protocol changes.
