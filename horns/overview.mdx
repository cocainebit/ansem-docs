# Horns

Horns are the hook layer on the ANSEM AMM. They are the ansemchain take on Uniswap v4 hooks: small contracts that bolt onto a graduated pool and run at swap time. When a token graduates from the [bonding curve](/launchpad/graduation) into the [AMM](/amm/overview), a Horn can be attached to its pool. From then on the Horn gets a say on every swap.

A Horn does one of two things:

1. **Skims rewards.** It diverts a slice of every swap's fee into the **Horn Vault**, where ANSEM and CHANSE stakers earn it.
2. **Reshapes the pool.** It changes how the pool prices, gates, or fills trades, for example a launch fee that starts high and decays down.

## How a Horn plugs in

The AMM asks the attached Horn `before_swap` and gets back one decision: **Proceed**, **Reject**, **OverrideFee**, or **Delta** (a price adjustment). It runs the swap, then calls the Horn `after_swap`. Two safety properties hold across every Horn:

- Any `Delta` a Horn returns is re-validated against live pool reserves by the AMM itself. This is the systemic backstop: **no Horn can drain a pool**, no matter what it returns.
- The reward-layer deposit rides as a reply-on-error sub-message, so a paused or broken Vault can never revert the underlying swap.

## Economics of the skim

The reward skim is a fraction **of the swap fee**, not of the trade. It is set in basis points of the fee:

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `skim_bps` | 2000 (20% of the fee) | Share of each swap's fee routed to the reward layer |
| Split | 5000 / 5000 (50/50) | How the skim divides between the ANSEM sink and the CHANSE sink |

Both are creator-set at launch and governable afterward. The remaining fee stays in the pool and compounds for liquidity providers exactly as it does on a hookless pool. See [Fee structure](/amm/overview#fee-structure) for the base swap fee the skim is taken from.

## Honest caveats

<Warning>
**A pool's hook is immutable at creation.** A Horn is chosen at launch and attached the instant the token graduates. It can never be added, removed, or swapped afterward. Only coins that graduate **after** the Horns were wired on ansem-1 carry a Horn; pools that graduated earlier stay hookless forever.
</Warning>

<Note>
Horn contract addresses change per genesis and are never hardcoded here. The launchpad and the app resolve them at runtime through the launchpad's `HornConfig` and `HornRegistry` queries. Always read the live wiring rather than assuming an address.
</Note>

## What is live on ansem-1

As of 2026-08-28, the in-place migration on ansem-1 made the AMM hook-aware and the launchpad Horn-aware. Live and wired:

- **Horn Vault** (staking, two sinks)
- **Fee-Share** (routes the skim into the Vault)
- **Fee Decay** (decaying launch fee)
- **Dynamic Fee** (condition-reactive fee)

The wider catalog of pricing and gating Horns becomes attachable as governance registers each one in the `HornRegistry`. See the [full catalog](/horns/catalog).
