# Withdraw: ansemchain to Solana

Withdrawing transfers a bridged asset from ansemchain back to Solana. When you submit the withdrawal, your coins are **escrowed** on ansemchain (not burned immediately). Once the relayer releases the matching SPL from that asset's pool vault on Solana, the escrow is settled and the denom's tracked supply is decremented.

You choose which asset to withdraw:

- **CHANSE** (`uchanse`) releases CHANSE SPL from the CHANSE vault
- **ANSEM** (`uansem`) releases ANSEM SPL from the ANSEM vault

Each asset has its own vault and its own limits.

## Prerequisites

- An ansemchain wallet (Keplr, Leap, or Ansem Wallet) holding the bridged asset you want to withdraw
- Your Solana wallet address (where you want to receive the SPL)
- A small amount of CHANSE for chain gas fees (~0.01 CHANSE) -- CHANSE is always the gas token on ansemchain, regardless of which asset you are withdrawing

<Warning>
Only bridge-minted balances can be withdrawn. Native staking rewards earned on ansemchain cannot cross back to Solana.
</Warning>

## Withdrawal amounts and admin approval

Each asset has an `admin_approval_threshold`. Withdrawals **at or below** the threshold are processed automatically. Withdrawals **above** the threshold are placed in `pending_approval` and require an admin `ApproveBurn` before the relayer releases funds on Solana. This is a safety control on unusually large withdrawals, not a fee or a rejection -- once approved, the withdrawal completes normally.

A withdrawal also cannot exceed the asset's `max_burn_per_tx`. See [Fees & Limits](/bridge/fees-and-limits) for the per-asset values.

## Step-by-Step Guide

<Steps>

### Connect your ansemchain wallet

Click **Connect Ansem Wallet** and select from Keplr, Leap, or Ansem Wallet. Your balances appear once connected.

### Choose the asset

Select **CHANSE** or **ANSEM**. The UI shows your balance for that asset, its `max_burn_per_tx`, and the `admin_approval_threshold` above which an admin approval is needed.

### Enter your Solana destination address

Paste the Solana wallet address where you want to receive the SPL. This is a standard Solana address (32-44 characters, Base58 encoded).

<Note>
You do not need to connect a Solana wallet for withdrawals -- just paste the address. The relayer handles the Solana-side transfer.
</Note>

### Enter the withdrawal amount

Type the amount you want to bridge back. The UI shows your available balance and warns you if the amount crosses the approval threshold.

Click **Max** to fill your full balance minus a small CHANSE gas buffer.

### Confirm the withdrawal transaction

Click **Withdraw** and approve the transaction in your ansemchain wallet. This submits `MsgBurnForBridgeOut`, which **escrows** your coins (they leave your spendable balance but are not burned yet). The UI will show:

1. **Approving Transaction...** -- Waiting for your wallet signature
2. **Broadcasting on ansemchain...** -- Submitting the escrow to the network

### Track your withdrawal

The **Withdraw Status Tracker** appears with your ansemchain transaction hash. If your amount was above the threshold, it shows **Pending admin approval** until an admin calls `ApproveBurn`. Otherwise the relayer proceeds directly.

</Steps>

## Processing Time

| Step | Duration |
|------|----------|
| ansemchain block confirmation | ~6 seconds |
| Admin approval (only if above threshold) | varies |
| Relayer detection | ~5-10 seconds |
| Solana withdrawal execution | ~5 seconds |
| **Total (auto-approved)** | **~20-30 seconds** |

## What Happens Under the Hood

```
1. You sign MsgBurnForBridgeOut on ansemchain (for uchanse or uansem)
2. The module ESCROWS the coins (does not burn yet) and assigns a unique burn_id
3. If amount > admin_approval_threshold → status = pending_approval
   An admin ApproveBurn is required before continuing
4. The relayer calls the Solana program's `withdraw` for that mint, releasing
   SPL from the matching pool_vault to your Solana token account
   (a ProcessedBurn PDA seeded on burn_id guards against double release)
5. The relayer submits MsgUpdateBurnStatus:
     completed → the escrow is burned and that denom's BridgeMintedSupply decremented
     failed    → the escrowed coins are refunded to you
```

## Limits

Withdrawals are subject to governance-controlled, per-asset limits:

| Limit | Scope |
|-------|-------|
| `max_burn_per_tx` | Per asset -- largest amount in one withdrawal |
| `admin_approval_threshold` | Per asset -- above this, an admin must approve |
| `max_burns_per_block` | Module-wide -- caps withdrawals per block |

See [Fees & Limits](/bridge/fees-and-limits) for current values. For amounts above `max_burn_per_tx`, split into multiple transactions.
