# Fees & Limits

The Ansem Bridge has no bridging fees -- you receive the exact amount you deposit or withdraw. You pay standard gas fees on each chain, and **per-asset** limits apply to deposits and withdrawals.

## Bridging Fees

| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Bridge fee | **None** (0%) |
| Deposit fee | **None** |
| Withdrawal fee | **None** |

You receive exactly the amount you bridge, minus only the gas fees for the transactions on each chain.

## Gas Requirements

You need gas tokens on each chain to execute bridge transactions. On ansemchain the gas token is always **CHANSE**, regardless of which asset you are bridging.

### Deposit (Solana to ansemchain)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| SOL for Solana tx | Sponsored by the relayer (no cost to user) |
| Account creation | First-time `UserAccount` rent also sponsored by the relayer (`register_sponsored`) |

Deposits are gasless: you can bridge in from a wallet with zero SOL. If the sponsor is briefly rate-limited, the bridge falls back to a self-paid transaction (then a tiny SOL balance is needed). See [Deposit](/bridge/deposit#no-sol-required-gasless-deposits).

### Withdraw (ansemchain to Solana)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| CHANSE for chain tx | ~0.01 CHANSE for the withdrawal transaction on ansemchain (CHANSE is the gas token even when withdrawing ANSEM) |
| SOL for Solana tx | Paid by the relayer (no cost to user) |

<Tip>
The withdrawal UI automatically reserves a small CHANSE buffer for gas when you click "Max".
</Tip>

## Per-Asset Limits

Each bridged asset is configured independently in the `x/bridge` module as a `BridgeAsset`. These are the fields that govern how much can move and when an admin must step in:

| Field | Applies to | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| `max_mint_per_tx` | Deposits | Largest amount minted from a single deposit for this asset |
| `max_burn_per_tx` | Withdrawals | Largest amount in a single withdrawal for this asset |
| `admin_approval_threshold` | Withdrawals | Withdrawals above this amount go to `pending_approval` and require an admin `ApproveBurn` |
| `enabled` | Both | Whether this asset can currently be bridged at all |

Every asset has its own values for each field. A limit that applies to CHANSE says nothing about ANSEM, and governance can tune each asset separately.

### Current Assets

| Asset | denom | Solana SPL mint | Notes |
|-------|-------|-----------------|-------|
| CHANSE | `uchanse` | Configured at deploy | Priority asset (`assets[0]`) |
| ANSEM | `uansem` | `9cRCn9rGT8V2imeM2BaKs13yhMEais3ruM3rPvTGpump` | 1:1 with the pump.fun mint |

<Note>
The exact numeric values for `max_mint_per_tx`, `max_burn_per_tx`, and `admin_approval_threshold` are governance parameters and can change per asset. The Bridge UI displays the live limit for whichever asset you select.
</Note>

### Module-Wide Limit

| Parameter | Value | Controlled By |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| `max_burns_per_block` | Caps how many withdrawals settle per block (across all assets) | Governance proposal |

## Why a large withdrawal needs approval

Withdrawals above an asset's `admin_approval_threshold` are not rejected -- they are held in `pending_approval` until an admin calls `ApproveBurn`, after which the relayer releases funds on Solana as normal. This is a safety control on unusually large outflows. Amounts at or below the threshold process automatically. To avoid the wait, keep each withdrawal at or below the threshold.

## Processing Time

| Step | Typical Duration |
|------|-----------------|
| Deposit (Solana to ansemchain) | ~30 seconds |
| Withdrawal, auto-approved (ansemchain to Solana) | ~20-30 seconds |
| Withdrawal above threshold | ~30 seconds + admin approval time |

Processing time includes network confirmation, finality waiting for deposits, and relayer execution.
