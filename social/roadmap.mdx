# What's next

SocialFi today already gives you signed identity, on-chain posts, and a full social surface. The direction from here is twofold: **move more of the stack on-chain**, and **wire social into the money** so that reach and holding reinforce each other.

<Note>
These are directions, not commitments or dates. They describe where the primitives already point.
</Note>

## More of the stack on-chain

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="On-chain profiles & handles" icon="id-card">
    Move the profile and handle registry into a contract, so your name and your address are bound by the chain, not a service.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-chain follow graph" icon="diagram-project">
    A follow graph anyone can read and build on: portable social, not locked inside one frontend.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Social wired into the money

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tips & paid posts" icon="coins">
    Tip a post in CHANSE or ANSEM, or gate a post behind a payment, settled by the same signed action that carries it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Token-gated posts" icon="lock">
    Rooms and posts visible only to holders of a given launchpad token, membership proven by balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Post to launch" icon="rocket">
    Turn a post into a token launch in one step, so a call and the coin it calls share one identity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reputation from history" icon="award">
    Reputation derived from a wallet's real on-chain history, holdings, trades, posts, instead of a follower count.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Social meets Horns

The [Horns](/horns/overview) already route a share of every graduated pool's fees back to holders. The natural next step is to let that value recognize contribution, so the people who show up, post, and bring flow are part of who the pool rewards. Social becomes an input to the economics, not a layer beside them.

Have something you want to see here? Bring it to a [proposal](/proposals/overview), the same governance that steers the rest of the chain steers this too.
