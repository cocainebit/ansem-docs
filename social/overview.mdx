# SocialFi

Social on ansemchain is built on one idea: **your identity is your wallet**. There are no usernames to squat, no accounts to create, and no server that can post as you. Every action, a post, a reply, a like, a repost, is signed by your wallet, and the signature is what proves authorship. The account you trade and hold with is the same account you post from.

This is what makes it *SocialFi* rather than social bolted onto a chain: the same address that owns your bags carries your posts, your reputation, and your reach.

## Why signed, not stored

On most apps, "you posted this" means "our database says you did." Here it means **the network can check the math**: each action carries a signature over its exact content, and the address is derived from the signing key, so authorship cannot be forged, reassigned, or edited after the fact by anyone, including the app.

Gas never gets in the way. A relayer sponsors the transaction, so posting costs you nothing and needs no CHANSE in your wallet, yet the relayer can only *carry* a message you already signed. It can never author one for you.
