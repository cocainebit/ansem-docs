# Welcome to Ansem

CHANSE is a token that grew its own blockchain. It lives in two places at once: as an SPL token on Solana, and as the native gas and staking asset of **ansemchain**, a zero-inflation proof-of-stake Layer 1. A bridge moves CHANSE between the two in about a minute.

The bridge is multi-asset, and CHANSE is its priority asset. It also carries **ANSEM**, a bridged 1:1 voucher for the pump.fun ANSEM token that trades on ansemchain alongside CHANSE. ANSEM is a tradeable asset only; gas and staking are always paid in CHANSE. More assets can be added over time.

On ansemchain, CHANSE powers a launchpad designed around one idea: **the rules that keep launches fair are enforced by contracts, not by promises.**

## What makes it different

- **Zero inflation.** Total CHANSE supply is fixed at 1 billion on Solana. The chain mints only what the bridge locks; supply across both chains is constant.
- **2.25% max wallet on every launchpad token**, enforced on chain by both the curve and the AMM. Nobody buys up a launch.
- **No price cliff at graduation.** The AMM pool opens at the exact price the curve ended at, with the reserved 20.69% of supply plus the entire graduation raise locked as liquidity forever.
- **Asymmetric fees** (0.5% buy / 3.5% sell) that favor conviction over churn. Every fee flows into the token's own liquidity, not to any creator or protocol cut.
- **Everything on chain.** Proposals are transaction memos. Tallies are recomputed from history. Apps, bots, and the wallet all read the same state.
