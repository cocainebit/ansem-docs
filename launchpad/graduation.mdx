# Graduation

Graduation is the moment a token outgrows its bonding curve and becomes a freely traded asset with permanent liquidity.

## The trigger

A token graduates when it reaches its **target market cap**. The target is derived from the oracle price (a target raised-USD threshold), with an optional fixed CHANSE override. The exact CHANSE raised at graduation therefore depends on the curve parameters and the CHANSE/USD price; with default parameters it works out to ≈ 25,700 CHANSE.

## What happens

<Steps>

### The triggering buy completes

The buy that reaches the target market cap executes normally; the buyer receives their tokens.

### The curve closes permanently

No further buys or sells can touch the curve.

### The AMM pool is created and seeded

The full CHANSE raised plus the reserved 20.69% of supply (20,690 tokens) are deposited as the pool's initial liquidity.

### The LP is locked forever

The seeded liquidity position can never be withdrawn. There is no rug: the raised-CHANSE floor is permanent.

</Steps>

## Price continuity

The pool opens at **the same price the curve ended at**. Curve buyers do not get marked down at graduation. By the numbers:

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Final curve price | Set by the curve's final state |
| Pool opening price | Identical to the final curve price |
| Graduation market cap | Set by the target (oracle-based) |
| Pool depth | Raised CHANSE + 20,690 tokens, locked |
| Liquidity token side as a share of supply | 20.69% |

## After graduation

The token trades on the [AMM](/amm/overview) with a 1% base swap fee. Anyone can add additional liquidity on top of the locked seed and withdraw their own share at any time; only the graduation seed is locked. The 2.25% max wallet continues to apply on every AMM swap.
