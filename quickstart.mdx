# Quickstart

The fastest path from nothing to holding a launchpad token.

<Steps>

### Install the wallet

Add the **Ansem** extension from the Chrome Web Store, create a wallet, and back up your seed phrase. Details: [Installing](/wallet/install).

### Get CHANSE on Solana

Buy CHANSE with SOL on its Solana market. Send it to your wallet's Solana address.

### Bridge to ansemchain

Open **Bridge to CHANSE** in the wallet, enter the amount, confirm. Your native CHANSE arrives in about a minute. Details: [Bridging](/wallet/bridging).

### Trade on the launchpad

Open the launchpad, pick a live token (or [create one](/launchpad/create-token)), connect the wallet, and buy on the curve. Your tokens appear in the wallet automatically, image and all.

### Optional: have your say

Browse [community proposals](/proposals/overview) and vote; it costs dust. Or do all of this from Telegram with the [bots](/telegram/overview).

</Steps>

## For developers

ansemchain is a proof-of-stake chain with CosmWasm smart contracts. Public endpoints:

| Service | URL |
|---|---|
| REST (LCD) | `https://rest.ansemchain.fun` |
| RPC | `https://rpc.ansemchain.fun` |

Start with the [CLI installation](/cli/installation), the [contract guide](/contracts/overview), or the [Chain API reference](/api/chain/introduction).
